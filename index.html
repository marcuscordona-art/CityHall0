About the Landmark

Philadelphia City Hall is the largest municipal building in the United States. It took 30 years to build and has over 14.5 acres of floor space.

Fun Fact: It was originally designed to be the tallest building in the entire world!

Key Features

  • The William Penn Statue: A 37-foot bronze statue of the city's founder stands at the very top.
  • The Observation Deck: Offers amazing 360-degree views of the Philly skyline.
  • The Materials: Built without a steel frame, using mostly brick, white marble, and limestone.
